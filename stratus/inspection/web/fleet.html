Separate ground context

A fleet keeps a frozen context until you attach another snapshot. One node is not 50 airborne sensors. Ground values do not change battery costs, detect obstacles, diagnose panel faults or establish wind/flight clearance. Original measurement times are retained during playback.

WHY THESE ASSIGNMENTS?

Allocation and attention

The bounded greedy rule assigns important, evidence-needed tasks without duplicate owners. It is not a globally optimal solver.

Allocation rounds and model details
AI may explain one compact fleet summary per request. It never executes aircraft actions. Live/cached/local numerical results remain independent of AI availability.


Save, reopen and report
Opening a saved fleet pauses playback. Budgets, pending events, reservations, evidence, seed and original ground times remain intact. Image assessments are authored replay awaiting human review.
